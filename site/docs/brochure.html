How it works

Four steps. About a minute.

1

Make your vault

Choose a passphrase. A few random words work well, like maple orbit quiet bicycle. Your vault is a single encrypted file. Nobody can read it without that passphrase, not even us.

2

Add a message

Paste one in, or import a WhatsApp chat export. People are added for you automatically and grouped as Work or Personal.

3

Make sense of it

Get a short summary, what they probably mean in plain words, how it sounds (with an honest confidence level), what they're asking, and three reply ideas written as you.

4

Save your vault

Press Save and your updated vault downloads as a file. Next time, open that file and carry on, exactly where you left off.

Files are named like axon-vault_r12_2026-10-07_1430.axon. The r12 is the revision, which goes up by one every time you save, and the rest is the date and time. Always open the highest number.

Keep your vault safe

  • Save often. The bar at the top says “Unsaved changes” until you do, and your browser warns you before you leave.
  • Keep two copies. One in your cloud drive or on a USB stick, and an older revision as a backup.
  • Use a phrase, not a word. Four random words are easier to remember and stronger than a short password.
  • On a shared computer, choose “Close vault” when you're done. It clears everything from memory.

Quick answers
What if I forget my passphrase?
It can't be recovered by anyone, which is what keeps your vault private. Choose something memorable, and keep an older copy of your vault as a backup.
Can I use it on my phone and my laptop?
Yes. Copy your .axon file to the other device and open it there. Carry one file forward at a time. The highest r-number is the newest.
Is the tone read always right?
No. It's a best guess from the words alone, so it shows its evidence and confidence. For anything important, you can always ask the person.
Does it work without internet?
Yes, once it has loaded once. Open your vault file (or your on-device copy) and read, search and explore your map. Only the AI parts need a connection.
Can I import my WhatsApp chats?
Yes. In WhatsApp choose Export chat → Without media, then import the file. Imported history stays in your vault and is never sent to an AI unless you open a message and ask.
